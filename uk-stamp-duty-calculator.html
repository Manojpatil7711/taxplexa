Stamp Duty for homes in England and Northern Ireland.

UK Stamp Duty rates by purchase date (2015–2026)

SDLT depends on the completion date, property price and buyer status. This calculator applies the published residential rate periods, including the temporary Stamp Duty holiday and the changes introduced on 1 April 2025. First-time buyer relief also has different price limits across periods. The additional-property surcharge changed from 3% to 5% on 31 October 2024.

  • 8 July 2020–30 June 2021: temporary £500,000 nil-rate threshold.
  • 1 July–30 September 2021: temporary £250,000 nil-rate threshold.
  • 23 September 2022–31 March 2025: £250,000 nil-rate threshold.
  • From 1 April 2025: standard £125,000 nil-rate threshold.

Official references: HMRC historical SDLT rates (2003–2025), HMRC Budget 2026 rates and allowances and current residential SDLT rules.

First-time buyer relief in 2018 generally meant 0% on the first £300,000 and 5% on the portion above £300,000 up to £500,000, provided every buyer met the eligibility rules and intended to occupy the home as their only or main residence. A purchase above £500,000 did not qualify for this relief.

Important: This is not one rate for the whole United Kingdom. Scotland uses Land and Buildings Transaction Tax (LBTT). Wales replaced SDLT with Land Transaction Tax (LTT) for transactions with an effective date on or after 1 April 2018. This calculator covers residential SDLT in England and Northern Ireland only.

Official historical sources: HMRC residential SDLT rates for 4 December 2014 to 7 July 2020, HMRC first-time buyer relief introduced in 2017 and HMRC SDLT to Welsh LTT transition guidance.

Frequently asked questions

How much was Stamp Duty in 2018?

For a standard home purchase in England or Northern Ireland, 2018 SDLT was 0% up to £125,000, 2% on the next £125,000, 5% on the next £675,000, 10% on the next £575,000 and 12% above £1.5 million. Eligible first-time buyers had different rates.

Did these 2018 rates apply across the UK?

No. Scotland uses LBTT. Wales replaced SDLT with Land Transaction Tax for transactions with an effective date on or after 1 April 2018. This calculator covers England and Northern Ireland only.
