UK Income Tax & Take-Home Pay Calculator

Salary after Income Tax and National Insurance (2026/27).

Rates used

This calculator uses the England, Wales and Northern Ireland rates: a £12,570 Personal Allowance, 20% basic rate up to £50,270, 40% higher rate up to £125,140 and 45% above that. The allowance reduces by £1 for every £2 earned over £100,000. Employee National Insurance is 8% between £12,570 and £50,270 and 2% above.

It does not cover Scottish rates, student loans, pension contributions or tax codes. Check gov.uk for your exact position.

Frequently asked questions

Does it work for Scotland?

No. Scotland sets its own Income Tax bands.

Is pension included?

No. Pension contributions can lower your tax bill.

Why might my payslip differ?

Tax codes, benefits, student loans and pension schemes all change the figures.
