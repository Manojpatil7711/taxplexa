Frequently asked questions

Does it work for Scotland?

No. Scotland sets its own Income Tax bands.

Is pension included?

No. Pension contributions can lower your tax bill.

Why might my payslip differ?

Tax codes, benefits, student loans and pension schemes all change the figures.

UK Income Tax calculator guide for tax years 2015/16–2026/27

Who this calculator is designed for

This estimator covers standard non-savings employment income for England, Wales and Northern Ireland. It lets you select a tax year and estimate Income Tax using the allowance and bands represented in the calculator. Scotland has separate non-savings Income Tax bands, so Scottish taxpayers should use the relevant Scottish rates and an appropriate calculator.

How to use it

  1. Select the tax year that matches the period you want to estimate. The UK tax year normally runs from 6 April to 5 April.
  2. Enter your gross annual salary in pounds, before Income Tax deductions.
  3. Calculate and review the estimated taxable income and tax due shown by the tool.
  4. Repeat with another tax year only when you want to compare historical rules; do not treat an old year's result as a current payslip estimate.

How the estimate works

The calculation first considers the Personal Allowance available for the selected year, then applies the relevant marginal tax bands to taxable income. The UK system is progressive: different slices of income can be taxed at different rates. Crossing a band threshold does not mean that all your income is taxed at the higher rate.

Why your payslip may differ

Your actual deductions can be affected by tax code adjustments, taxable benefits, pension contributions, salary sacrifice, student-loan repayments, savings or dividend income, multiple jobs, bonuses and changes during the year. National Insurance is separate and is not included in this Income Tax estimate. Residence, eligibility for allowances and special reliefs can also change the result.

Check official information

Tax rules and thresholds can change. Confirm the selected year's figures with GOV.UK Income Tax rates and allowances and HMRC guidance before making payroll or filing decisions. This calculator is an educational estimate, not tax advice or a substitute for HMRC calculations.
