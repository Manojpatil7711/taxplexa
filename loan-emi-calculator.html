Loan & Mortgage Calculator

Monthly payment, total interest and cost of any loan.

How this calculator works

It uses the standard amortisation formula: your monthly payment stays fixed, while the share going to interest falls and the share going to the loan balance rises over time. Enter the loan amount, the yearly interest rate and the term in years.

Try changing the term. A longer term lowers the monthly payment but increases the total interest you pay.

Frequently asked questions

Does it include fees?

No. It covers interest and principal only. Add arrangement fees and insurance separately.

Is the rate fixed?

It assumes one rate for the whole term. If your rate changes, run the calculation again for each period.

Can I use it for a car loan or personal loan?

Yes. Any loan with equal monthly repayments works.

Loan EMI calculator: a practical guide

When should you use this calculator?

Use this tool before applying for a home loan, car loan, education loan or personal loan. It helps you compare repayment periods and understand how the interest rate affects the monthly instalment. It is an estimate for loans repaid in equal monthly instalments; it is not a lender quotation or approval.

How to enter your loan details

  1. Loan amount: enter the principal you expect to borrow, not the total price of the asset if you will pay a deposit.
  2. Annual interest rate: enter the yearly percentage quoted by the lender. Check whether the quote is fixed, variable, nominal or an APR-style figure.
  3. Term: enter the repayment period in years. A longer term usually lowers the monthly instalment but increases the total interest.
  4. Select the currency that matches your loan and calculate. Compare the monthly payment, total repayment and interest together.

Formula and worked example

For a standard reducing-balance loan, EMI = P × r × (1 + r)n ÷ ((1 + r)n − 1), where P is principal, r is the monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100), and n is the number of monthly payments. For example, a loan of 120,000 at 12% annually for 12 months has an estimated monthly payment of about 10,661.85, before fees or insurance. The exact result depends on rounding and lender terms.

How to compare loan offers responsibly

Compare the same principal and term across lenders. Review processing fees, insurance, early-repayment charges, late-payment penalties and whether the rate can change. Ask for the lender's amortisation schedule and total cost of credit. Do not choose a loan only because its monthly payment is lower: extending the term can make borrowing more expensive overall.

Limitations

This estimate assumes regular monthly payments and one constant rate. It does not include fees, payment holidays, changing rates, irregular repayments or lender-specific day-count rules. Use the lender's formal repayment schedule before signing an agreement.
