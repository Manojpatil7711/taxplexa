Loan & Mortgage Calculator

Monthly payment, total interest and cost of any loan.

How this calculator works

It uses the standard amortisation formula: your monthly payment stays fixed, while the share going to interest falls and the share going to the loan balance rises over time. Enter the loan amount, the yearly interest rate and the term in years.

Try changing the term. A longer term lowers the monthly payment but increases the total interest you pay.

Frequently asked questions

Does it include fees?

No. It covers interest and principal only. Add arrangement fees and insurance separately.

Is the rate fixed?

It assumes one rate for the whole term. If your rate changes, run the calculation again for each period.

Can I use it for a car loan or personal loan?

Yes. Any loan with equal monthly repayments works.
