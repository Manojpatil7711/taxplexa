Free invoice generator: create a clear customer invoice

Who can use this invoice tool?

Freelancers, independent professionals, small businesses and sellers can use this browser-based tool to prepare a simple invoice for a customer. It helps organise seller details, customer details, line items, quantities, prices and a tax rate, then print or save the result as a PDF. It does not register a business or submit an invoice to a tax authority.

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Choose the currency that matches the transaction.
  2. Enter the seller's name and contact or business details exactly as they should appear.
  3. Enter the customer's name and billing details. Check spelling and address carefully.
  4. Add a unique invoice number and the correct invoice date.
  5. Add each product or service as a separate line, with a clear description, quantity and unit price. Use Add item for additional lines.
  6. Enter a tax rate only when it applies to your transaction and you are entitled or required to charge it.
  7. Select Create invoice, review every field, then use Print or save as PDF.

What makes an invoice useful?

A good invoice identifies who supplied the goods or services, who the customer is, what was supplied, when it was supplied, the amount due, currency and payment terms. Depending on the country and tax status, it may also need a tax registration number, tax breakdown, supply date, sequential invoice number or other prescribed wording. Add payment instructions and a due date where appropriate.

Before sending the PDF

Check arithmetic, quantity, unit price, discounts, tax treatment, customer details and invoice number. Keep a copy for your records and follow your local rules for numbering, corrections, retention and credit notes. If you are not registered for VAT or GST, do not add tax merely because the field is available.

Privacy and limitations

The tool is designed to create the invoice in your browser and does not require an account. Avoid using a shared or public device for confidential customer information, and clear the browser session when finished if necessary. The tool is not accounting software: it does not send invoices, track payments, calculate tax liability, validate registration numbers or guarantee compliance with local law. Check official requirements or ask an accountant before issuing tax invoices.
