What is compound interest?

Compound interest means you earn interest on your earlier interest. This calculator adds interest every month and then adds your monthly deposit, so you can see how steady saving builds over time.

The result is an estimate. Real returns vary, and investment returns are not guaranteed.

अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले प्रश्न

How often is interest added?

Monthly, with deposits added at the end of each month.

Does it include tax or inflation?

No. Results are before tax and are not adjusted for inflation.

Is the growth guaranteed?

No. Use a cautious rate when planning.
