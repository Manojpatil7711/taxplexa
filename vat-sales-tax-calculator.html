VAT & Sales Tax Calculator

Add or remove VAT, GST or sales tax from any price.

Adding and removing tax

To add tax, multiply the net price by the rate. To remove it, divide the gross price by 1 plus the rate. The UK standard VAT rate is 20%. Enter any rate to use this for GST or sales tax in other countries.

Frequently asked questions

What is the UK VAT rate?

The standard rate is 20%. Some goods have a reduced or zero rate.

Can I use it for GST?

Yes. Enter the GST rate for your country.

Why remove tax from a price?

To find the net amount for accounting or invoicing.

VAT and sales tax calculator: add tax or find the net price

When to add or remove tax

Use the add-tax mode when you know the pre-tax price and want to estimate the amount payable after tax. Use remove-tax mode when you have a tax-inclusive price and need to work backwards to the net amount. This calculator accepts a rate you enter, so it can support different VAT, GST or sales-tax examples; it does not decide which rate legally applies to a product or country.

How to use the calculator

  1. Select the currency used in the transaction.
  2. Enter the amount. In add mode this is the net price; in remove mode it is the gross, tax-inclusive price.
  3. Enter the applicable tax rate as a percentage, such as 20 for 20%.
  4. Choose whether to add tax or remove tax, then calculate.

Formula with a simple example

To add tax: tax amount = net price × rate ÷ 100; gross price = net price + tax amount. For a net price of 100 and a rate of 20%, tax is 20 and the gross price is 120. To remove tax from a gross price: net price = gross price ÷ (1 + rate ÷ 100). A gross price of 120 at 20% therefore contains 20 tax and 100 net value.

Choosing the correct rate

Tax rates can vary by country, product category, customer type, place of supply and transaction date. Some goods or services may be zero-rated, exempt or subject to reduced rates. Do not assume the standard rate applies to every item. For UK transactions, check GOV.UK VAT rates and the relevant HMRC guidance. For India, use the GST-specific tool and official GST rate information.

Limitations

This is a mathematical calculator, not a VAT return or tax-compliance system. It does not determine registration obligations, place-of-supply rules, input-tax recovery, exemptions, rounding rules or invoice requirements. Confirm the treatment with the relevant tax authority or a qualified adviser.
