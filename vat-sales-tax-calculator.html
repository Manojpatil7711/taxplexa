VAT & Sales Tax Calculator

Add or remove VAT, GST or sales tax from any price.

Adding and removing tax

To add tax, multiply the net price by the rate. To remove it, divide the gross price by 1 plus the rate. The UK standard VAT rate is 20%. Enter any rate to use this for GST or sales tax in other countries.

Frequently asked questions

What is the UK VAT rate?

The standard rate is 20%. Some goods have a reduced or zero rate.

Can I use it for GST?

Yes. Enter the GST rate for your country.

Why remove tax from a price?

To find the net amount for accounting or invoicing.
