How Stamp Duty is calculated

You pay each rate only on the slice of the price that falls in that band, not on the whole price. This tool uses 0% to £125,000, 2% to £250,000, 5% to £925,000, 10% to £1.5m and 12% above. First-time buyers pay 0% to £300,000 and 5% on the next slice up to £500,000, and the relief does not apply above £500,000. Additional properties add a 5% surcharge to each band.

Wales (LTT) and Scotland (LBTT) use different taxes. Always confirm figures with gov.uk or your solicitor.

वारंवार विचारले जाणारे प्रश्न

Does it cover Scotland or Wales?

No. They have their own property taxes.

What counts as an additional property?

Usually a second home or buy-to-let. Rules have exceptions, so check gov.uk.

When do rates change?

Governments can change thresholds at any Budget.
