यूके स्टॅम्प ड्युटी (SDLT) कॅल्क्युलेटर

इंग्लंड व नॉर्दर्न आयर्लंडमधील घरांवरील स्टॅम्प ड्युटी.

UK Stamp Duty rates by purchase date (2015–2026)

SDLT depends on the completion date, property price and buyer status. This calculator applies the published residential rate periods, including the temporary Stamp Duty holiday and the changes introduced on 1 April 2025. First-time buyer relief also has different price limits across periods. The additional-property surcharge changed from 3% to 5% on 31 October 2024.

  • 8 July 2020–30 June 2021: temporary £500,000 nil-rate threshold.
  • 1 July–30 September 2021: temporary £250,000 nil-rate threshold.
  • 23 September 2022–31 March 2025: £250,000 nil-rate threshold.
  • From 1 April 2025: standard £125,000 nil-rate threshold.

Official references: HMRC historical SDLT rates (2003–2025), HMRC Budget 2026 rates and allowances and current residential SDLT rules.

वारंवार विचारले जाणारे प्रश्न

Does it cover Scotland or Wales?

No. They have their own property taxes.

What counts as an additional property?

Usually a second home or buy-to-let. Rules have exceptions, so check gov.uk.

When do rates change?

Governments can change thresholds at any Budget.
