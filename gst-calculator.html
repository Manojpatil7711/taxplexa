GST Calculator (India)

Enter the GST rate that applies to your item and supply date. Choose intra-state or inter-state to see the CGST/SGST or IGST breakdown.

How GST is calculated

For an intra-state sale, GST is split equally into CGST and SGST. For an inter-state sale, the tax is charged as IGST. When adding tax, GST equals the net price multiplied by the rate. When removing it, divide the gross price by 1 plus the rate.

Indian GST history and rate guidance

India's GST took effect on 1 July 2017; it did not apply in 2015/16 or 2016/17. GST rates depend on the HSN/SAC classification, the goods or service, and the transaction's time of supply. Enter the rate confirmed for your item and date; this calculator does not guess a universal rate.

Major rate revisions recommended by the 56th GST Council meeting took effect for most goods and services on 22 September 2025, with specified exceptions. Check the latest official notifications before filing.

GST calculator India: understand CGST, SGST and IGST

What this calculator helps you do

This tool estimates GST on a taxable value or extracts GST from a tax-inclusive amount. It also shows the usual tax split for an intra-state or inter-state supply. The correct rate depends on the goods or service classification, applicable notification and time of supply; the calculator does not automatically identify the HSN or SAC rate.

How to use the inputs

  1. Select Indian rupees (or the currency relevant to your example).
  2. Enter the taxable amount for add mode, or the GST-inclusive amount for remove mode.
  3. Enter the GST percentage confirmed for the item and transaction date.
  4. Select intra-state when the supply is within the same state, or inter-state when it is between states, then calculate.

CGST + SGST versus IGST

For a typical intra-state taxable supply, the total GST is split equally into Central GST (CGST) and State GST (SGST), or UTGST where applicable. For an inter-state supply, Integrated GST (IGST) is generally charged instead of a CGST/SGST split. The place-of-supply rules can be more complex for services, imports and special transactions, so verify the classification before invoicing.

Worked example

If the taxable value is ₹1,000 and the applicable total GST rate is 18%, the GST amount is ₹180 and the invoice total is ₹1,180. For a standard intra-state example, this would usually be ₹90 CGST and ₹90 SGST. For an inter-state example, it would usually be ₹180 IGST. This example explains the arithmetic only; it does not say that 18% applies to your item.

Check the official rate before using it

GST rates and exemptions depend on HSN/SAC classification and effective dates, and notifications may change. Check the official GST Portal and CBIC GST resources for current notifications and guidance. Keep the invoice, classification and rate evidence for your records.

Limitations

This tool does not validate GSTINs, determine place of supply, calculate input-tax credit, prepare returns or decide whether a supply is exempt or zero-rated. It is a calculation aid, not filing or legal advice.
