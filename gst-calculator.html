GST Calculator (India)

Add or remove GST from any price. Common rates are 5%, 18% and 40%.

How GST is calculated

To add GST, multiply the net price by the rate. To remove it, divide the price that includes GST by 1 plus the rate. For sales within one state, GST is split equally into CGST and SGST. For sales between states, it is charged as IGST.

Rates depend on the goods or service. Check the current rate on the official GST portal before filing.

Frequently asked questions

Which GST rates apply?

Common rates are 5%, 18% and 40%, and some items are exempt. Confirm your item's rate on the official GST portal.

Can I use it for VAT?

Yes. Enter the VAT rate of your country.

Why remove tax from a price?

To find the net amount for accounting or invoicing.
